Install PouchLog

On iPhone/iPad

  1. Tap Share (arrow icon).
  2. Choose 'Add to Home Screen'.

PouchLog runs full-screen without browser chrome.

On Android

  1. Open the menu in the browser.
  2. Pick 'Install app' or 'Add to Home screen'.

After install you'll find PouchLog in your apps.
